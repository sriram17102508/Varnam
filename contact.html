FAQS

Commonly Asked

How does Varnam curate its vendors and venues?
▼
Every partner listed on Varnam goes through a strict verification process. We evaluate their portfolio quality, guest reviews, past delivery speed, pricing transparency, and business registration. Only the top 10% of luxury service providers in each region make it onto our directory.
Is the wedding planning dashboard free to use?
▼
Yes, Varnam's core planning tools—including the budget estimator, guest list manager, checklist, and basic wedding website templates—are entirely free for couples. Premium customized designer templates and concierge services are available under bespoke upgrades.
How do I setup my wedding microsite?
▼
Simply navigate to your dashboard and look for the 'Your Wedding Microsite' option. Add your story, select from our stunning themes (such as Palace, Boho, Minimalist, or Fairytale), input your schedule details, and click 'Publish'. Your microsite becomes active instantly under a premium layout.
How do I register as an organizer or vendor?
▼
You can click the 'Vendor Registration' option in the footer of any page. Fill out your business credentials, starting prices, and portfolio link. Our curation committee will review your submission and notify you via email within 24 to 48 hours once approved.
