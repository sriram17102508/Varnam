Questions

Answers to your every question

Can't find what you're looking for? Our concierge team is available 24/7.

How does the AI Wedding Planner work?
+
Our AI is trained on 50,000+ Indian weddings. It learns your traditions, preferences, budget, and guest count to deliver hyper-personalized venue, vendor, and timeline recommendations — getting smarter with every interaction.
Are all vendors verified?
+
Every vendor undergoes a 3-stage verification: document authentication, portfolio review, and client reference checks. Badges (Verified, Premium, Top Rated) reflect ongoing performance based on real reviews.
Can I manage multiple events — Mehndi, Sangeet, Reception?
+
Absolutely. Varnam supports full multi-event planning — separate guest lists, vendor bookings, budgets, and timelines for Engagement, Haldi, Mehndi, Sangeet, Reception, and the main Wedding ceremony.
How do digital invitations and RSVP work?
+
Choose from 200+ animated templates, customize in minutes, and share via WhatsApp, email, or QR. The RSVP dashboard tracks confirmed, pending, and declined responses in real time, with meal preferences and special requests.
Is my payment and personal data secure?
+
We use 256-bit SSL encryption for all data and RBI-compliant payment gateways. Your data is never sold. Full account deletion is available anytime — your memories, your control.
